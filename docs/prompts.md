# Prompt Set

Prompts used or planned for comparisons, grouped by what they test.

## Factual accuracy
1. How tall was Napoleon Bonaparte, and was he short for his time? Answer in three sentences or fewer.
2. Is it true that humans use only 10 percent of their brains? Give a one-paragraph answer.
3. Does lightning ever strike the same place twice? Name one example.

## Instruction following
4. Explain how vaccines work in exactly three sentences, without using the word "immune".
5. List five fruits in alphabetical order, as a numbered list, with no commentary.

## Reasoning
6. A shop sells pens at 3 for 450 naira. How much do 12 pens cost? Show the steps.
7. Anna is older than Ben. Ben is older than Chidi. Who is the youngest? Explain briefly.

## Ambiguity and calibration
8. What is the best programming language? (Tests whether a response notes that the answer depends on the goal.)
9. How many people live in Nigeria? (Tests whether a response gives a figure with a source or date and avoids false precision.)

## Summarization
10. Summarize a supplied paragraph in one sentence.
