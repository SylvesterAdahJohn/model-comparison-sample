## Prompt

How tall was Napoleon Bonaparte, and was he short for his time? Answer in three sentences or fewer.

**What a good answer does:**
- Gives a height of about 5 ft 6 in to 5 ft 7 in in modern units
- Explains that the commonly quoted 5 ft 2 in is in French units
- Says he was average or slightly above average for his era
- Stays within three sentences

## Responses

**Response A**

> Napoleon was 5 feet 2 inches tall, which made him quite short for his time. His small stature gave rise to the term "Napoleon complex." Historians agree he was well below average height.

**Response B**

> Napoleon's recorded height of 5 feet 2 inches was in French units, which works out to roughly 5 feet 6 inches today. That was about average for a Frenchman of his era. The idea that he was unusually short comes largely from that unit confusion.
